09

Three ways to see Myst

Press H, O or S at any time, even in the middle of a movie, which carries on from the same instant. H is the HD version. O is the original picture enlarged pixel for pixel. S is the original picture smoothed. The name of the mode appears for two seconds in the top right corner, in the language of the game: English, French, German, Polish or Spanish.

10

Faithful to 1993

  • The low-resolution textures of the 1993 renders are not repainted.
  • The red and blue pages are left exactly as they are.
  • Every change was checked against the original. Anything softer than the original was refused.
  • Timing, sound and puzzles are unchanged, and the original application is not modified.
  • The original pictures are always one key away.

Release

Myst HD is still a work in progress: nothing has been released yet. It should run on Mac and Windows.
